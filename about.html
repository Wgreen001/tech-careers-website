About this site

A beginner-friendly guide to careers in information technology.

Why it exists

Choosing a path in IT can feel overwhelming. This site gives each role its own page so you can compare them in one place.

Who it is for

Students, career changers, and anyone curious about working in technology.

What you will learn

For each career: what the job involves, the skills and tools used, typical pay, certifications, and how people move up.
